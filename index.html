Enter your NAICS codes

The federal award data is already loaded. Type the codes you compete in and the analysis runs against a prepared dataset — no download, no file to upload.

Prepared by MakeGov from the Federal Procurement Data System and SAM.gov entity registrations, already rolled up to parent companies. Only the codes you type are sent — no file is uploaded and nothing about you is transmitted. Codes measured by employee count cannot be analyzed, because headcount does not appear in award data.
You are viewing this inside a frame. The analysis will run normally, but browsers block file downloads from framed pages, so the download buttons at the end may do nothing.
Before you start. This tool estimates from federal contract dollars. Size standards are based on total company revenue, including commercial work, subcontracts, and affiliated companies — none of which appear in this data. Results are a list of candidates worth investigating, never a determination of who is small. Not legal advice.
Prefer to use your own USAspending extract?

Use this if you need a scope, date range, or reporting-delay cutoff the prepared dataset does not cover. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded.

Drop your file here or click to browse — .zip or .csv
Which file to download from USAspending. Award Search → Advanced Search → Download → Custom Award Data. Choose Contracts and Prime Award Transactions, not the award summaries. Set the date range to five full federal fiscal years (October 1 through September 30).

Draft a comment to SBA

Agencies must respond to significant comments. Sentiment is not significant; a position backed by data is. Choose the position you want to take, and the tool drafts a comment around the figures from your run. The output is an unbranded Word document you can paste into your own letterhead. It runs entirely in your browser — no account, no sign-in, nothing sent anywhere.

The tool takes no position on the rule. The analysis measures; it does not argue. The position below is yours, and the argument sections are written to it. Read the draft before you file it — the words are submitted under your name, not ours.

Companies in the band

Read this before using any of these numbers. This tool measures federal contract dollars. Size standards measure total company revenue — commercial work, subcontracts, and affiliated companies all count toward a size determination and none of them appear in this data. A company can look small here and be well over the threshold in reality. What you have is a ranked list of candidates worth investigating, not a determination of who is small.
